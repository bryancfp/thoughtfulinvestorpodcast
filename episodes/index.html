About the Podcast

Better investing starts with better questions.

The Thoughtful Investor is a Yach Advisors podcast hosted by Bryan Yach, CFP®. The show explores investing, financial planning, market history, behavioral finance, investor psychology, risk, diversification, valuation, patience, incentives, and the limits of what any of us can know about the future.

The goal is simple: help listeners become more thoughtful about the way they make decisions with money.
