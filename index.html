About the Podcast

Investing deserves more than headlines and hot takes.

The Thoughtful Investor is about understanding why we make the financial decisions we do — and how better thinking can lead to better outcomes.

Markets are noisy. Financial information is everywhere. Yet more information does not always create better investors.

The Thoughtful Investor explores the ideas behind successful investing and financial planning: risk, uncertainty, valuation, behavior, diversification, patience, incentives, and the limits of what any of us can know.

The goal is not to predict the next market move. It is to become better at thinking about money.
